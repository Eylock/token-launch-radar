How the ranking is built

The snapshot holds 758 protocols at or above $5M of TVL, fetched 2026-11-14. A protocol is rankable when its record carries no token (market cap, CoinGecko id and symbol all empty) and it is not a wrapped, staked or bridged product of a token that already trades. That gives protocols — the 40 on the house watchlist plus every other rankable one, above or below the $50M floor. Protocols whose token already trades are set aside; they are not ranked, because there is no launch left to value.

  1. Category multiple. The median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the snapshot's own live-token protocols in the same category (at least 5 comparables, otherwise the overall median of ), capped at 1.0. Lending trades near 0.13× TVL, bridges near 0.56–0.75× — a flat multiple would price them as the same business.
  2. Growth. One week of TVL change, clamped to −60%…+120% and half-weighted, so a single hot week cannot carry the whole score.
  3. Maturity. 0.5 for a newborn, rising to 1.0 over four years with no token — survival is evidence.
  4. Breadth. +5% per extra chain, capped at ten, because distribution widens the future holder base.
